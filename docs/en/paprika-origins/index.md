# Paprika Origins Compared: China, India & Spain

**TL;DR** — Origin shapes variety and flavour, not quality. Chinese paprika (Xinjiang sweet
pepper, Yidu red) is the colouring workhorse; Spanish pimentón is prized for smoked flavour;
Indian chilli spans a wider heat range. Compare on specification — ASTA, SHU, flavour — not on
origin alone.

## The Three Origins at a Glance

| Origin | Typical profile | Signature use |
|---|---|---|
| China | High-colour sweet pepper (Xinjiang), Yidu red | Natural red colouring, industrial paprika |
| India | Wide heat range, chilli-dominant | Heat-focused chilli products |
| Spain | Smoked pimentón varieties | Flavour-driven paprika, charcuterie |

## China: The Colouring Workhorse

China is the world's largest chilli producer — roughly 55% of global production — and its paprika
is dominated by high-colour, low-heat sweet pepper varieties:

- **Xinjiang sweet pepper / Red Dragon 23** — under 500 SHU, ASTA 8–18, for food colouring and
  paprika oleoresin extraction
- **Yidu red / Wangdu** — under 10,000 SHU, high colour, for low-heat paprika and marinades

The scale matters. Because China grows chilli across five distinct producing regions — from the
North China Plain to the Northwest colour-pepper belt in Xinjiang and Gansu — a buyer can source
both the colouring paprika and the heat-focused chilli from one supply base. Chinese paprika is
the standard choice when the goal is extractable red colour without heat. See
[chilli varieties & grades](../chilli-varieties/index.md) for the full variety map.

## India: Heat Across the Spectrum

India is the other top producer, but its chilli profile skews toward heat rather than pure
colour — a wide range of varieties from mild to very hot. Buyers sourcing from India typically
specify heat level (SHU) as the primary criterion.

## Spain: Flavour-First Pimentón

Spain produces a smaller volume but is renowned for **pimentón** — paprika made from peppers
smoke-dried over oak fires. The result is a distinctive smoky flavour that is as much a flavour
ingredient as a colouring one. Pimentón is the choice for charcuterie, stews and flavour-driven
products, not for maximum extractable colour.

## How to Choose

The origin decision follows the job:

| Goal | Best origin choice |
|---|---|
| Maximum extractable red colour | China (high-ASTA sweet pepper) |
| Target heat level | China or India (match variety to SHU) |
| Smoked flavour | Spain (pimentón) |

The common mistake is judging quality by origin. A high-ASTA Chinese paprika and a smoked Spanish
pimentón are both "quality" products — they simply solve different problems. Specify colour value,
heat level and flavour, then match the origin to the specification. See
[ASTA colour & SHU explained](../asta-shu-explained/index.md).
