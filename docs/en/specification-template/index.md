# Buyer Specification Templates

**TL;DR** — Copy the block for your product form, replace each bracket with your target
value, and send it with your enquiry. A complete specification names variety (or SHU window),
origin, form, mesh, moisture, water activity, SHU, ASTA colour and the food-safety parameters
for your market — then verify with a lot-specific COA.

## Template 1 — Chilli powder

```
Product:        Chilli powder
Variety:        [Sanying / Zidantou / Jixin — or state SHU window only]
Origin:         [Shandong]
SHU window:     [20,000–50,000]
ASTA:           [≥100]
Mesh:           [60–80 mesh]
Moisture:       [≤13%]
Water activity: [≤0.65]
Food safety:    [EU aflatoxin limits · pesticide · microbial per market]
Packaging:      [25 kg kraft bags, export cartons]
Documents:      [lot-specific COA · food-safety certificates · traceability]
```

## Template 2 — Chilli flakes / crushed

```
Product:        Chilli flakes / crushed chilli
Variety:        [Jinta / Xian — visible flakes, bright colour]
Origin:         [Gansu]
SHU window:     [10,000–20,000]

Food safety:    [aflatoxin · pesticide · microbial per market]

Origin:         [province]
SHU window:     [–]
ASTA:           [–]
Mesh:           [20–40 flakes · 60–80 powder · 100+ sauces]

```

## Why a written specification pays for itself

Heat, colour, texture, safety and shelf life each drift on their own when unspecified. A
one-line spec costs nothing to write and saves a failed batch — the buyers who get consistent
product are the ones who specify all of it and then verify with a lot COA.

Bracketed values are representative examples from our buying guide — confirm your exact SHU,
ASTA, moisture and market limits with us before contracting.
