# Applications & Formulation

**TL;DR** — Paprika and dried chillies serve two different jobs in food: paprika colours,
chilli heats. Many formulations combine both — paprika for a stable red colour, chilli for the
target heat profile. Matching the right variety to the job is what separates a consistent
product from a variable one.

## Where Paprika Is Used

Paprika's primary role is natural red colouring, with mild flavour:

- **Spice blends and seasoning mixes** — colour and mild warmth
- **Marinades and meat rubs** — colour with clear chilli aroma
- **Sausage and processed meat** — natural red colouring
- **Snack and instant-noodle seasoning** — colour base
- **Paprika oleoresin extraction** — the feedstock for natural red food colour

## Where Dried Chillies Are Used

Dried chillies add heat, with colour as a secondary benefit:

- **Hot sauces and chilli oil** — high-heat powder or flakes
- **Snack seasoning** — high-heat powder for punch
- **Seasoning blends** — heat level matched to target profile
- **Food service and hot-pot** — whole dried chillies for visible heat
- **Processed meat and marinades** — medium heat and aroma

## The Colour + Heat Combination

The most useful formulation pattern is combining paprika and chilli:

| Goal | Use |
|---|---|
| Stable red colour, no heat | Sweet paprika only |
| Red colour + mild warmth | Low-heat paprika |
| Heat with some colour | High-heat chilli powder |
| Heat + colour, layered | Paprika (colour) + chilli blend (heat) |

A chilli blend itself often layers multiple varieties — a high-heat variety for punch, a medium
variety for body, and a high-colour paprika for visual appeal. This "layered heat and colour"
approach is how consistent hot sauces and seasonings are built.

## Formulation Notes

- **Heat is non-linear** — a small amount of extra-hot chilli (50,000+ SHU) moves a blend's heat
  far more than the same weight of medium chilli.
- **Colour is the base** — for shelf-stable red, specify paprika ASTA and confirm sterilisation,
  since colour shifts in storage (see [ASTA colour & SHU explained](../asta-shu-explained/index.md)).
- **Mesh affects release** — finer powder releases colour and heat faster than coarse flakes.
