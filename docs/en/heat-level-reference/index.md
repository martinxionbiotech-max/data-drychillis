# Chilli Heat Level Reference

**TL;DR** — One table covers the heat of every major Chinese chilli variety, from sweet pepper
(under 500 SHU) to Jixin (50,000–70,000 SHU). Start from your target SHU, find the band, then
name the variety in your specification.

## The Complete Heat Table

This is the reference table for matching a variety to a target heat profile. SHU values are
representative for each variety; confirm the exact window with your supplier.

| Variety | Heat (SHU) | Heat band | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xinjiang sweet pepper, Red Dragon 23 | <500 | Sweet | Paprika powder, food colouring |
| Yidu red, Wangdu | <10,000 | Low heat | Low-heat paprika, flakes, marinades |
| Xian pepper | 10,000–20,000 | Medium | Medium powder, chilli paste |
| Jinta | 10,000–20,000 | Medium | Medium powder, flakes |
| Erjingtiao | 10,000–20,000 | Medium | Chilli paste, aroma |
| Sanying / Tianying | 20,000–50,000 | High | Hot sauce, snack seasoning |
| Zidantou (bullet pepper) | 20,000–50,000 | High | Hot sauce, industrial food |
| New Generation | 20,000–50,000 | High | Hot sauce, seasoning |
| Seven-Star | 20,000–50,000 | High | Snack seasoning, hot-pot |
| Xiaomi | 30,000–50,000 | Extra hot | Extreme heat, chilli oil |
| Jixin (chicken-heart) | 50,000–70,000 | Extra hot | Extreme heat, Sichuan dishes |
| Chilli King, Shuan Shuan | >50,000 | Extra hot | Extreme-heat products |

## The Four Heat Bands

| Band | SHU range | Sensation | Product forms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sweet / low heat | <500 – 10,000 | Gentle warmth | Paprika powder, flakes |
| Medium heat | 10,000–20,000 | Noticeable heat | Powder, flakes, paste |
| High heat | 20,000–50,000 | Strong, lingering heat | Powder, flakes, sauce base |
| Extra hot | >50,000 | Intense whole-mouth burn | Powder, segments, chilli oil |

## Why Precise SHU Matters

Heat is non-linear: a small amount of extra-hot chilli (50,000+ SHU) moves a blend's heat far
more than the same weight of medium chilli. That is why specifying a **precise SHU window** —
not just "hot" or "medium" — is what keeps a hot sauce or seasoning consistent batch to batch.
See [ASTA colour & SHU explained](../asta-shu-explained/index.md) for the chemistry behind the
number.

## How to Pick a Variety

1. **Start from target SHU** — find your heat band in the table above
2. **Check the fruit type** — whole chilli, flake, powder and segment orders need different fruit
   shapes (see [chilli varieties & grades](../chilli-varieties/index.md))
3. **Name the variety** — write "Sanying, 20,000–50,000 SHU" not "hot chilli powder"

Heat is only one of three factors — colour (ASTA) and flavour vary independently, so pair this
table with the full variety guide when building a specification.

## What Buyers Should Ask

1. **What SHU window** — start from your target heat, then find the band.
2. **Which variety** — the table maps variety to SHU; name the variety, not the band.
3. **What else matters** — colour and flavour are separate; confirm both alongside heat.
